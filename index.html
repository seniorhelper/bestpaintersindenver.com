Questions people ask

Who are the best painters in Denver?

There is no honest single answer, and any site that crowns one painter for every house is selling you something. The best painter for your home is the one who will put the standard in writing: the prep steps, the primer, the products and sheens for each surface, how the site is protected and cleaned, and a written workmanship warranty. Painter Hotline puts all of that in your estimate and backs the work with a 5-year workmanship warranty.

What do top-rated painters do differently?

Mostly they do more before the first coat: they protect floors and furniture, repair cracks and dents, sand glossy spots, spot-prime repairs and stains, and choose a primer that fits the surface. Then they cut straight lines by hand, keep coverage even, and clean up every day.

Do you offer virtual estimates?

Yes. Send photos and a few measurements for a virtual estimate, or ask for an in-person estimate at the house. You choose.

Which paint brands do you use?

We paint with all major brands, including Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr, Valspar and PPG. The product and sheen for each surface are written into your estimate.
