About

A studio for people who notice the edges

This is Painter Hotline’s Denver color and finish studio: where we show how we work, help with color, and book the projects that deserve extra care.

About us in briefPainter Hotline is a Denver-headquartered painting company with specialist crews for interiors, exteriors, cabinets, commercial spaces, and fences and decks. Over 20 years of experience, fully insured, with a written 5-year workmanship warranty.

Why a site called “Best Painters in Denver”?

Because it’s the question people ask, and most answers are lists. We think a better answer is a set of standards you can check, so that’s what this site is. We don’t claim a ranking, and we won’t put up reviews that aren’t ours.

What we believe

  • Prep is the job. Paint is the last thin layer on top of everything that came before it.
  • Color is light. A color is only right in the room it’s going in.
  • Do it right the first time. We’re often hired to fix other painters’ work. We would rather nobody needed to.
  • Write it down. The scope, products, sheens and warranty go on paper before the work starts.
  • Estimates your way. Virtual from photos, or in person. Your choice.

The brands we use

We paint with all major brands, including Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr, Valspar and PPG, and name the product for each surface on your estimate.
