Questions people ask

How soon will you reply?

We reply to consult requests by phone or email, usually within one business day. If it's urgent, call or text 720-208-5645.

What should I have ready for a virtual consult?

Daylight photos of each room from the doorway, close-ups of floors and counters, and which way the main windows face. Reply to our email with the photos.

Can I bring my Color Studio palette?

Yes. Use the 'Book a consult with this palette' button in the Color Studio and your palette is added to this form automatically.
