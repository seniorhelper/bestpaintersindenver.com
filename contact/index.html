Questions people ask

How do I get a painting estimate?

Call or text 720-208-5645, or use the form. Choose a virtual estimate from photos or an in-person estimate at the house.

What photos help with a virtual estimate?

One wide photo of each room or wall from the doorway, close-ups of any damage, and rough measurements or the ceiling height. Daylight photos work best.
