Questions people ask

Are these photos from your own projects?

Yes. Every photo in this gallery comes from Painter Hotline crews. We don't use stock photography of finished rooms.

Why show prep photos instead of only finished rooms?

Because the finished room only looks right for years if the prep was done right. Protection, repairs and primer are the parts you can't see later, so we show them now.
